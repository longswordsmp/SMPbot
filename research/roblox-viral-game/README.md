## Sourcing caveat

The research ran inside a sandbox whose network policy blocked `roblox.com`, the Roblox DevForum and Creator Hub, the fan wikis, and every concurrent-player tracker. Two agents also exhausted their search quota mid-task.

**Most figures therefore come from search-engine result snippets rather than primary pages.** The exception is Roblox's own creator documentation, which was reachable via its GitHub mirror and is the most reliable material here. Every claim in the appendices is tagged for provenance and single-sourced claims are marked unverified.

Section 7 of the strategy brief lists the ten things to re-verify from an unblocked network before any of this drives a spending decision.
