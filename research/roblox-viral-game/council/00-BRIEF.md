# Council Brief: SKYTREE

This is the shared briefing for the four-agent council. All four agents read this same document.

---

## The concept under evaluation

**SKYTREE** — working title.

**The one-line pitch:** You and your friends grow one enormous tree together over two real-world weeks, and what it becomes depends on what you all fed it.

### How it works

**The Grove.** You belong to a persistent Grove of up to 20 players. The Grove is the social unit, backed by a Roblox Community. It survives across sessions, unlike a server.

**The Tree.** Your Grove has exactly one Skytree. It is enormous, it is visible from anywhere on the map, and it grows in real time whether or not anyone is logged in. Growth is faster when more Grove members are actively tending it.

**The minute-to-minute loop.** Gather water, light and nutrients from around the map. Carry them up to the tree. Feed it. Fight off blight, beetles and parasites that attack the trunk and roots. Prune dead growth. Each of these is a simple physical action a seven-year-old understands on sight.

**The two-week arc.** A Skytree takes roughly ten to fourteen real-world days to mature. You watch it get visibly, dramatically bigger over that period. You cannot rush it with money.

**The payoff.** What it matures into depends on what the Grove collectively fed it. There are dozens of tree species — a Glass Tree, a Bone Tree, a Clock Tree, a Storm Tree. The species is the collection. When it matures it bears fruit, which is the currency and the collectible.

**The reset.** You harvest, plant a seed from the mature tree, and begin again with a permanent bonus. This is the rebirth mechanic. Seeds from rare trees carry better bonuses.

**Cross-pollination.** You can visit other Groves. Rare species only become possible through pollen exchanged between two Groves that each grew something unusual. This forces inter-group contact.

### Why this shape was chosen

1. **The multi-day timer is itself the Day 8-28 retention system.** It is not a bolted-on weekly league. The core mechanic physically requires you to return on day eight and day fourteen, because that is when your tree matures.
2. **Co-play is mandatory, not encouraged.** A solo player grows a tree far too slowly to reach a good species. "Intentional co-play" is a headline signal in Roblox's June 2026 discovery algorithm.
3. **The intellectual property is entirely owned.** Original tree species, no borrowed meme characters. The Mementum v. Do Big Studios litigation has made borrowed meme IP a live liability.
4. **Content is nearly free.** A tree species is a mesh and a texture treatment. Same economics as the mutation systems that power the incumbents.
5. **The lane is empty.** Every formula game on Roblox makes the other players antagonists. No significant title makes them mandatory collaborators.

### The honest weaknesses, stated up front

- Nothing is stolen, so there is less drama than the incumbent formula.
- Two weeks is a very long time horizon for the core demographic.
- "Waiting" games can feel empty between the beats.
- Monetization is harder when you cannot sell a way to skip the wait, and skipping the wait would destroy the entire design.
- No proven comparable in this exact shape, which cuts both ways.

## Market facts the council must reason against

All figures are from the research corpus in `research/roblox-viral-game/appendices/`. Most were gathered from search-result snippets because the sandbox blocked Roblox's own sites and all player-count trackers. Treat them as directionally reliable and individually uncertain.

### Platform state

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Daily active users, Q2 2026 | 123M, down from 132M in Q1 |
| Bookings growth | Fell from +43% to +8% year on year |
| Q3 2026 guidance | −14% to −18%, the first decline ever |
| Full-year guidance | Withdrawn |
| Mobile share of activity | 72–80% |

Management attributed the collapse to "a greater-than-expected shift in engagement away from high-monetizing viral games and the impact of discovery algorithm changes."

### The discovery algorithm

- June 2026: Recommended For You moved from a 7-day to a 28-day retention window, scored in Day 1, Day 2-7 and Day 8-28 buckets.
- "Intentional co-play" is a headline signal.
- August 2026: Roblox began testing an additional reward for games strong on both retention and monetization together.
- Stated goal: stop games that "win a quick click but don't offer deeper substance."

### Reference cases

- **Grow a Garden**: built in three or four days by an anonymous 16-year-old, launched March 2025, peaked at 22.3M concurrent in August 2025, reportedly earned about $12M in May 2025 alone. By September 2026 it was down to roughly 63,000 concurrent.
- **Steal a Brainrot**: launched May 2025 on a licensed concept, peaked at 25.8M concurrent in October 2025, estimated $11M per month in mid-2025. By September 2026, roughly 366,000 concurrent.
- **Animal Hospital**: co-op veterinary anomaly horror, launched May 2026, exceeded 700,000 concurrent, swept the RDC 2026 Innovation Awards including Best New Game and People's Choice. No "Steal a" or "Grow a" title won anything.
- **Plants vs Brainrots**: 8.65M concurrent peak, proving the tower-defense hybrid lane.
- **Murder Mystery 2**: all-time peak of 1,349,555 in August 2026, showing evergreen round games are resurgent.

### Production assumptions

- Two people, eight weeks, under $1,000 in assets.
- Launch marketing: $150–700 in Roblox Ads Manager plus optionally $1,000–5,000 per mid-tier YouTuber.
- Roblox bears all hosting, moderation and payment costs.
- The cold start for the 2025 and 2026 mega-hits was YouTube coverage, not paid advertising.

### Risk environment

- A federal multi-district litigation consolidating 182+ child-safety cases was created in December 2025.
- State attorneys general in six states have sued; about $54M in settlements paid.
- August 2026: media-feed, autoplay and watch-to-earn mechanics barred from the Roblox Kids and Select tiers.
- Paid random items must disclose exact odds summing to 100%.
- Fourteen clones of a viral idea appeared within 48 hours in a documented 2026 case.

## The four roles

1. **The Believer** — Build the strongest possible case that Skytree works. Who specifically plays it, who spends money and why, and what the upside looks like if it lands.
2. **The Skeptic** — Attack it. Why players will not pay, why they will not return, and every weakness not already listed above.
3. **The Investor** — Ignore whether it is a good game. Model whether it makes money, with explicit arithmetic, and judge the realism of the opportunity against alternatives.
4. **The Judge** — Read all three. Deliver one verdict.
