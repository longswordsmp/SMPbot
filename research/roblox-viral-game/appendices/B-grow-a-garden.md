## 1. Origin and history

### 1.1 Creation
- **Creator:** an anonymous 16-year-old Roblox user with the username **"BMWLux"**. The first version was reportedly built in about **three days**. [S: growagarden.fandom.com/wiki/Jandel; en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grow_a_Garden]
- **Release date:** **March 26, 2025**, as a free-to-play multiplayer idle game on Roblox. [S: Wikipedia; allthings.how]
- The game began under the working name/placeholder "The Garden Game" (the Roblox Wiki page is titled "The_Garden_Game/Grow_a_Garden"). [S: roblox.fandom.com URL slug] [BK: the experience was renamed to "Grow a Garden" shortly after launch — unverified]
- Weekly updates began in March 2025 and became the game's operating rhythm (Saturday updates). [S: gamespot.com "Next Grow A Garden Update"; growagarden.fandom.com/wiki/Admin_Abuse]

### 1.2 Acquisition by Splitting Point Studios (April 2025)
- **Buyer:** **Splitting Point Studios**, the New Zealand studio founded in 2017 by **Janzen "Jandel" Madsen**. [S: gameanalytics.com Splitting Point profile; rnz.co.nz]
- **When:** **April 2025**, when the game had roughly **1,000 concurrent users (CCU)**. [S: growagarden.fandom.com/wiki/Jandel; Wikipedia]
- **Stake:** Splitting Point acquired **50% co-ownership**; **BMWLux kept 50%** ("According to Jandel, the original developer owns 50% of the game"). [S: growagarden.fandom.com/wiki/Jandel; Wikipedia; calcalistech.com]
- **Price:** **Not publicly disclosed.** No source in this session gave a dollar figure; several explicitly say the financial terms were kept private. Treat any number circulating online as **unverified**. [S: gamepressure.com; sportskeeda.com; Wikipedia]
- **Do Big Studios** (Bonita Springs, Florida) then joined as a **development and distribution partner** holding a **minority share**; the game is described as "co-owned by its original developer BMWLux and Splitting Point Studios" with Do Big as minority partner. [S: Wikipedia; legalclarity.org]
- A recurring 2025 news cycle asked "Did Jandel sell Grow a Garden?" (gamepressure.com, sportskeeda.com, noleep.com). The summaries obtained confirm only the April 2025 50/50 structure and do not document any later full sale; **any subsequent change of control is unverified**. [S: gamepressure.com; sportskeeda.com]

### 1.3 Who runs it now (as of the latest data found)
- Day-to-day the game is fronted by **Jandel** (Roblox/TikTok handle @jandelrblx), who personally runs the "Admin Abuse" live events and announces update delays (e.g., Bizzy Bees 2026 "delayed from the original May 2 date by Jandel"). [S: growagarden.fandom.com/wiki/Admin_Abuse; tiktok.com/@jandelrblx; gagdata.com patch notes]

This most likely reflects a restructuring into a new entity rather than the studio ceasing to exist (Splitting Point signed the November 2025 film deal and shipped 2026 content), but the reason is **unverified**. ## 2. Core loop in detail

### 2.1 The loop in one paragraph
Buy seeds from a globally synchronized **Seed Shop** whose stock rotates every **5 minutes**; plant on your personal plot; wait (plants keep growing **even while you are offline**); harvest fruit; sell it at the sell stand for **Sheckles** (the in-game currency); reinvest in rarer seeds, **gear** (sprinklers, watering cans), and **eggs** that hatch **pets** with passive buffs; chase **mutations** (weather-, pet- and gear-driven value multipliers that stack) to make individual fruits worth millions; **trade** with other players; show up for **Admin Abuse** streams where the owner spawns rare weather and hands out exclusives. [S: roblox.com game description "plant seeds, harvest (or steal), and watch your world bloom"; growagarden.fandom.com Seed_Shop; pcgamesn.com mutations; multiple wikis]

### 2.2 Offline growth — the key innovation
- Plants continue growing while the player is logged out, so returning players find grown crops (and, with mutations, big windfalls). This is what makes the game an "idle" game rather than a session-based farm sim, and it is the reason to log in daily and around update/admin-abuse windows. [S: Wikipedia classifies GAG as a "multiplayer idle video game"] [BK: crops that are fully grown persist; multi-harvest plants regrow; the combination of offline growth with a 5-minute shop timer is widely credited as the retention engine — unverified interpretation]

### 2.3 Seed Shop and stock rotation
- **Restock timer:** ~every **5 minutes** (Grow a Garden 2 sources say "approximately every 4 to 5 minutes"), on a **global timer synchronized across all servers** — everyone sees the same stock at the same time. [S: nerdschalk.com; growagarden2.net; gag.gg]
- Stock is **shared globally, but purchases are client-side**: one player's buying does not reduce stock for others. [S: growagarden.fandom.com/wiki/Seed_Shop]
- **Paid restock:** **79 Robux** (or watch an ad in GAG2) to refresh the shop instantly. [S: nerdschalk.com / gag2 stock sites]
- **Daily Deals** section runs on a separate **24-hour** cycle. [S: nerdschalk.com]
- Rarity tiers (Common → Uncommon → Rare → Legendary → Mythical → Divine → Prismatic) with rarer seeds having low per-restock appearance chances; "Divine seeds" are a recognized tier with dedicated guides. [S: mygagcalculator.com "Divine Seeds" URL; BK for tier names]
- **Prices [BK, unverified in session — treat as approximate; Ember Lily's 779 Robux figure is corroborated by [S]]:** Carrot 10 Sheckles; Strawberry 50; Blueberry 400; Orange Tulip 600; Tomato 800; Daffodil 1,000; Corn 1,300; Watermelon 2,500; Pumpkin 3,000; Apple 3,250; Bamboo 4,000; Coconut 6,000; Cactus 15,000; Dragon Fruit 50,000; Mango 100,000; Mushroom 150,000; Grape 850,000; Pepper 1,000,000; Cacao 2,500,000; Beanstalk 10,000,000; Ember Lily 15,000,000; Sugar Apple 25,000,000; Burning Bud 40,000,000; Giant Pinecone 55,000,000. Robux prices for the same seeds run from single digits (Carrot ~7) to ~900+ (e.g., Ember Lily **779 Robux ≈ $7.79** [S]).
- Sheckles start tiny (new players begin with a couple dozen Sheckles [BK]) and the top-end seeds are priced in the tens of millions, so the progression curve spans ~7 orders of magnitude.

### 2.4 Gear Shop
- Runs on the **same 5-minute global clock** as the Seed Shop. [S: nerdschalk.com]
- Typical items [BK, unverified]: Watering Can (~50,000 Sheckles; speeds growth), Trowel (~100,000; move plants), Recall Wrench (~150,000), Basic/Advanced/Godly/Master Sprinklers (~25,000 / 50,000 / 120,000 / 10,000,000; growth speed, size and mutation chance), Favorite Tool (~20,000; protects fruit from being sold), Harvest Tool (~30,000), Lightning Rod (~1,000,000; forces Shocked mutation during Thunderstorm), Friendship Pot (~15,000,000). Gear is also sold for Robux.
- A **"Grandmaster Sprinkler"** and a **"Premium Pass"** exist as premium items (Premium Pass is resold on third-party sites like itemku, June 2026 listing). [S: itemku.com]

| Egg | Sheckles | Robux | Shop appearance chance | Hatch time | Pet pool |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Common Egg | 50,000 | 19 | 100% | 10 min | 3 pets |
| Legendary Egg | 3,000,000 | 129 | 12% | 4 h | 5 pets |

[S: gamerant.com egg guide; msn.com mirror]

- Pets have hunger (e.g., the 2026 flagship **Empress Bee** is Divine rarity with **250K hunger**), passive abilities (growth speed, mutation chance, Sheckle bonuses) and can themselves receive pet mutations. [S: gagdata.com patch notes (May 2026)]
- **Hatch odds are shown in-game as percentages** (the game's egg UI lists per-pet chances) [BK]; critics nevertheless describe eggs as loot boxes "with unknown odds" [S: medium.com/@alvarlaigna] — see §6.

### 2.6 Mutations (the value engine)
Mutations are multipliers applied to a fruit's sell value. As of 2026 guides count **158–165 distinct mutations**. [S: gagdata.com]

Key mutations and multipliers:
| Mutation | Multiplier | How obtained |
|---|---|---|
| Wet | ×2 | Rain or Thunderstorm weather |
| Chilled | ×2 | Frost weather |
| Frozen | ×10 | Wet + Chilled combined (Frost on wet fruit) |
| Gold | ×20 [BK] | Rare random chance on harvest (~1% [BK]); Dragonfly pet |
| Rainbow | ×50 | Rare random chance (~0.1% [BK]) |
| Shocked | ×100 | Thunderstorm lightning strike; Lightning Rod |
| Celestial | ×120 | Meteor Shower |
| Voidtouched | ×135 | Black Hole / admin event |
| Abyssal | ×240 | 2026-era event mutation |
| Pollinated | ×3 | Bees (Bizzy Bees) |
| HoneyGlazed | ×5 | Honey / Bizzy Bees 2026 |
[S: pcgamesn.com; gagdata.com; growagarden.miraheze.org; beebom.com; gagdata.com patch notes for Pollinated/HoneyGlazed]

- **Stacking formula (additive over 1):** total = (Growth mutation: Gold ×20 or Rainbow ×50) × (1 + Σ(each environmental multiplier − 1)). Example from a guide: Shocked (×100) + Frozen (×10) = **×109, not ×1,000**. [S: gagdata.com mutations]
- Sources of mutations: **Weather, Pets, Sprinklers, Gear.** [S: growagarden.miraheze.org]

### 2.7 Weather events
| Weather | Frequency | Duration | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rain | ~every 20 min (most common) | 3–5 min | Wet |
| Thunderstorm | every 2–6 h | 3–5 min | Wet; lightning → Shocked |
| Frost | periodic (was removed during Summer Harvest, later restored) | 3–5 min | Chilled/Frozen |
| Night | every 4 h (made permanent in Bizzy Bees update) | >5 min | Moonlit [BK] |
| Blood Moon | 33% chance when Night triggers | — | Bloodlit [BK] |
| Meteor Shower | rarest natural weather, "once every few nights" | 3–5 min | Celestial |
| Bee Swarm | hourly during Bizzy Bees | — | Pollinated |
| Rainbow / Flood | shortest | 1–2 min | — |
[S: growagarden.fandom.com/wiki/Weather; droidgamers.com; beebom.com; mygagcalculator.com]
- Many high-value weathers (e.g., Black Hole, Volcano, Tornado, Disco [BK]) are **admin-only**, spawned during Admin Abuse.

### 2.8 Admin Abuse (owner-run live events)
- Recurring event, typically **Saturdays** around major updates, plus **"Watering Wednesday"**. Jandel uses admin powers to **spawn special weathers, restock shops, give players exclusive seeds/pets or "Admin Packs"**, and talk to the whole player base via a global message system. [S: growagarden.fandom.com/wiki/Admin_Abuse]
- Timing: in 2025 it usually started **~1 hour before** the update; by 2026 **~30 minutes before**. Players are told to be online early. [S: same]
- **Admin Abuse War (Aug 23, 2025):** on **Aug 15, 2025** Jandel "declared war" on Steal a Brainrot, alleging SpyderSammy copied Grow a Garden ideas (mutations, Admin Abuse). Both games ran simultaneous admin-abuse events at **10 AM EST on Aug 23, 2025**; creators including **KreekCraft, MrBeast and PokeDaily** promoted it. Result: **Grow a Garden 22.3M vs Steal a Brainrot 20.3M** — a combined ~42M CCU across two games. Jandel's on-stream nickname "Scammy Sammy" stuck. [S: stealabrainrot.fandom.com Admin_Abuse_War; growagarden.fandom.com Admin_Abuse_War; youtube.com full-event uploads]
- Note the irony: both "warring" games are Do Big Studios–linked (Do Big holds a minority stake in GAG; SpyderSammy is Do Big's Chief Product Officer — see §4).

### 2.9 Trading, cosmetics, Sheckles economy, and Grow a Garden 2
- **Trading** is a core meta-activity; a large Discord ecosystem exists for trades, "stock" alerts, and middleman services. Bloomberg (May 30, 2025) reported an "underground digital fruit economy" — real-money trading of crops and pets that "appears to violate company rules." [S: bloomberg.com 2025-05-30; top.gg / disboard.org listings]
- **Cosmetics shop** refreshes ~every **4 hours** (GAG2). [S: nerdschalk.com]
- A **Season Pass** system exists by 2026 (Season 4 launched April 4, 2026). [S: gagdata.com patch notes]
- **Grow a Garden 2:** early access **June 12, 2026**; official release **10:00 AM PT / 1:00 PM ET / 7:00 PM CET**, with **one-hour early access** for players who connected their Roblox account on the official GAG2 early-access page and **invited five friends** via link (a built-in referral loop). GAG2 keeps the same shop architecture (5-min seed/gear, ~30-min eggs, ~4-h cosmetics, 79-Robux restock, ad-based restock). A wave of fake "dupe glitch" scripts/scams followed launch. [S: beebom.com; games.gg; nerdschalk.com; allthings.how dupe article]

## 4. The studio playbook: Splitting Point, Do Big, and the "game factory" model

### 4.1 Splitting Point Studios (New Zealand)
- Founded **2017** by **Janzen Madsen ("Jandel")**, who discovered Roblox at a friend's party. [S: gameanalytics.com]
- Pre-GAG track record: **50+ games**, **6.5B+ visits**; flagship **Field Trip Z** drew **45M unique players** in one year. Team grew "from 10 to over 50" (GameAnalytics profile); Datanyze lists **29 employees**. [S: gameanalytics.com; datanyze.com]
- Janzen Madsen has a **Forbes profile page** (forbes.com/profile/janzen-madsen) — likely a 30 Under 30 listing [BK, unverified]. [S: forbes.com]
- Signed the **Story Kitchen film deal (Nov 5, 2025)** as the rights holder. [S: bloomberg.com; deadline.com]
- NZ entity dissolved 20 Aug 2025 per company registry (see §1.3) — probable restructure, unverified.

### 4.2 Do Big Studios (Florida)
- **HQ:** Bonita Springs, Florida. **Founded May 20, 2020** as "Do_Big Games" by CEO **John Cannata** (Roblox: dbs_johnny). Described as "an American gaming development, acquisitions, and marketing corporation" and "among the top three largest publishers and creators on the platform." [S: roblox.fandom.com Do_Big_Studios; crunchbase.com]
- **Model:** "acquires, partners with, and owns many Roblox experiences" — buys high-potential titles, then scales them with "aggressive growth and monetization strategies." Provides analytics, marketing (creator/YouTuber relationships), and publishing support. [S: roblox.fandom.com; grokipedia.com]
- **Portfolio (verified from search summaries):** **Grow a Garden** (minority), **Blade Ball**, **a dusty trip**, and formerly **Fisch** — on **Aug 21, 2025** Do Big returned Fisch's ownership to creator WoozyNate. [S: roblox.fandom.com]
- **Steal a Brainrot link:** **SpyderSammy (Sam Brakta)**, creator of Steal a Brainrot, is Do Big Studios' **Chief Product Officer**. [S: youtube.fandom.com/wiki/SpyderSammy; stealabrainrot.fandom.com]
- Do Big exhibited at **gamescom latam 2026**. [S: b2b.latam.gamescom.global]

- **Games the brief asked to verify:**
  - *Bubble Gum Simulator Infinity* — made by **BIG Games** (Preston "Rice"), a **different** studio from Do Big Studios; the similar names cause confusion. [BK — the search results returned BIG Games' community page, not a Do Big link]

### 4.3 How the machine runs (synthesis)
1. **Scout and buy early:** Splitting Point bought 50% at ~1,000 CCU (April 2025) — weeks before the game exploded — and kept the teen creator on as co-owner. [S]
2. **Bring in a publisher-operator:** Do Big supplies distribution/marketing muscle (creator network, cross-promotion, analytics) for a minority stake. [S]
3. **Weekly Saturday update cadence + owner-run "Admin Abuse" hype windows** that spike CCU on a predictable schedule; the CCU records all fell on Saturdays (Jun 21, Jul 19, Aug 23). [S]
4. **Global synchronized shop timers** (5 min) that give players a reason to be in-game continuously and make "stock" a social/Discord topic. [S]
5. **Creator-economy amplification:** MrBeast, KreekCraft and PokeDaily promoted the Admin Abuse War; the game is a top TikTok/YouTube content generator. [S]
6. **Manufactured rivalry as marketing:** the Jandel-vs-Sammy "war" pulled 42M+ concurrent players across two Do Big-linked games in one hour. [S]
7. **Franchise extension:** film deal (Story Kitchen, producers behind Sonic the Hedgehog / Tomb Raider adaptations) and a sequel (**GAG2**, June 2026) with a referral-gated early access. [S]
8. **Live-ops calendar in 2026:** Easter (Apr 4) → Bizzy Bees (May 9) → Summer Camp (June) → GAG2 (June 12), with Jandel personally announcing delays. [S]

## 5. The "Grow a ___" clone wave

- **Direct Roblox clones found:** **Grow a Brainrot Garden** (place id 9171617162) — "buy seeds when they restock, plant, wait, collect profits"; **Brainrot Garden!** (place id 134126723742818). [S: roblox.com listings]
- **Outside Roblox:** Bloxd.io's **"Grow & Farm"** (formerly "Farming-Simulator"), created **July 25, 2025** by S0MPENDER, explicitly "inspired by Grow a Garden"; it was removed from Bloxd's front page and demoted to custom games on **Oct 3, 2025**. [S: bloxd-io.fandom.com]
- **Web knockoffs:** grow-agarden.io and steala-brainrot.io host browser imitations. [S]
- YouTube creators produced "I played every Grow a Garden clone" / "Ranking every clone worst to best" videos, indicating dozens of copies. [S: youtube.com]
- **The "Steal a ___" wave:** Steal a Brainrot (SpyderSammy, 2025) adopted GAG-style mutations and Admin Abuse and out-grew GAG by late 2025; Jandel publicly accused it of copying. [S: stealabrainrot.fandom.com]
- [BK, unverified]: 2025 also saw a flood of "Grow a Tree", "Grow a Fish", "Grow an Egg", "Grow a Zoo/Ranch/Farm" experiences; the sequence "Grow a → Steal a → Plants vs Brainrots → 99 Nights" defined Roblox's 2025 front page. None of these matched GAG's scale, and by 2026 the Roblox charts were dominated by Steal a Brainrot and GAG2 rather than the clones.
- **Fate summary:** the clone wave mostly died within weeks (typical Roblox pattern); the notable survivors were not clones but "same-shaped" successors (Steal a Brainrot) and the official sequel.

## 6. Criticism, regulation, and platform response

### 6.1 Monetization / gambling concerns
- **Former Square Enix executive** (Jacob Navok [BK]) called the game "somewhat evil": "Do you wanna pay to grow this faster? Do you wanna pay to steal this from someone's garden?" — describing it as "incredibly similar" to gambling and pay-to-win aimed at children. [S: gamesradar.com]
- Alvar Laigna (Medium): "Roblox's Grow a Garden: A Predatory Scam Targeting Kids" — cites Ember Lily at 779 Robux, boosts at 375 Robux, paid stealing, loot-box eggs "with unknown odds," FOMO from timed stock. A counter-essay ("Response to Claims About Grow a Garden from a Game Dev and Casual Player") argues the mechanics are standard F2P. [S: medium.com]
- Roughly **40% of Roblox's player base is under 13**; critics argue egg/loot mechanics are "a couple of steps away from literal gambling." [S: medium/GamesRadar summaries]
- Bloomberg's "underground digital fruit economy" story documented **real-money trading** in violation of Roblox rules. [S: bloomberg.com 2025-05-30]
- "The Algorithm Behind Steal a Brainrot" (Substack) and similar pieces frame the GAG/SAB genre as engagement-optimized "brainrot." [S: freesystems.substack.com]

### 6.2 Regulatory and legal angles (Roblox-level, not GAG-specific)
- **Roblox MDL:** the Judicial Panel on Multidistrict Litigation consolidated child-exploitation suits in **December 2025**; **162 pending cases as of June 2026**; issues include parental controls, age verification, adult–minor contact, moderation. [S: lawsuit-information-center.com; consumernotice.org]
- **Florida AG James Uthmeier** issued **criminal subpoenas** to Roblox in **October 2025**; multiple states are investigating safety claims. [S: robertkinglawfirm.com; litigationconnect.com]
- **Addiction lawsuits** against Roblox Corp are being filed by parents. [S: robertkinglawfirm.com]
- Aftermath: "Lawyers look to consolidate dozens of Roblox child exploitation lawsuits" and "Brainrot Goes To Court [Correction]" — the latter concerns litigation touching Steal a Brainrot; **details not retrievable** in this session. [S: aftermath.site]
- **No lawsuit specifically targeting Grow a Garden, Splitting Point or Do Big Studios was found.** Grow a Garden appears in legal-marketing pages only as an example of Roblox's scale. [S]

### 6.3 Roblox's response
- Roblox executives publicly celebrated GAG as an example of "organic discovery" (Q2 2025 call) rather than distancing from it. [S: earnings summaries]
- [BK, unverified in session]: Roblox's Paid Random Items policy requires odds disclosure, and GAG's egg UI does show hatch percentages; Roblox rolled out facial age-estimation for chat (late 2025/early 2026) and expanded parental spending controls under AG pressure. These are platform-wide measures, not GAG-specific.
